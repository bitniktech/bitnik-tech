Privacy

Updated October 23, 2026

This site has no accounts, no advertising, no tracking pixels and no newsletter. It sets no cookies of its own.

The contact form

When you press Send, the name, email, project type and message you typed go to our server, which emails them to us. We use them only to reply to you. We don't add you to a mailing list or share them with anyone.

The form is delivered by Cloudflare, which hosts this site and sends the email on our behalf. The message then sits in our Google Workspace inbox like any other email.

Fonts

The typefaces load from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads.

Hosting

Cloudflare serves this site and, like any web host, briefly processes your IP address to deliver pages and stop abuse.
