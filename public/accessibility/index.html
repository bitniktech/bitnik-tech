Accessibility

Updated October 23, 2026

We build to WCAG 2.2 Level AA, the same standard we use for client sites.

What that means here

  • Headings and landmarks structure every page, and every page has a skip link.
  • Everything works from the keyboard, with a visible focus outline. The hold-to-engage button also responds to Enter or Space.
  • Text contrast meets AA against the dark background.
  • Every screenshot has a text description.
  • When your device asks for reduced motion, the spinning planet, the image reveals and the scroll animations stop.
  • No sideways scrolling at phone, tablet or desktop widths.

Known limits

The work section shows screenshots of our apps and websites. Each has a text description, but the words inside a screenshot can't be read by a screen reader or resized on their own. The spinning planet is decorative and hidden from screen readers.
